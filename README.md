The core pillars of this web app are rooted in technology, narrative, and empathy.

The charts are not simply visualizations, they are stories waiting to be understood. They ask What happened? Why did it happen? What are the consequences? And, most importantly, what can we do?

The integration of LLMs further enriches the experience. They do more than explain what the charts show. They invite curiosity, encourage exploration, and create a deeper sense of engagement between the user and the story behind the data.

Empathy is woven into the experience through different audience perspectives. I want users to see the same information through different lenses - General, Explain Like I’m Five, or Scientist. Each lens reflects a different way of understanding, because people do not all arrive with the same knowledge, context, or questions. After all, empathy is a language that brings clarity.

And when technology, storytelling, and empathy come together, the charts become more than charts, they become a way of seeing, understanding, and connecting with information.
